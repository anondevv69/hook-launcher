### P0 — direct wallet↔pool System transfers admitted as `trade-transfer` — FIXED (fixture 32)

The review was right, and the underlying reason is stronger than "the DBC shape doesn't use one": **no** venue in this codebase has an exact format that requires a direct wallet↔pool SOL transfer. Both venues are WSOL-based (wrap → venue swap → close); the house venue's swap is likewise funded from the wallet's WSOL account. The `'trade-transfer'` admission is therefore **deleted outright** — for a System transfer to the pool (buy) or from the pool (sell), classification now admits only the WSOL wrap into the wallet's own WSOL ATA. Token-program wallet↔pool transfers remain admitted (they are part of real shapes) and remain backstopped by send-time effect accounting, which bounds the pool's token gain by the approved `amountIn` and the wallet's token loss the same way. Fixture 32 appends a direct wallet→pool transfer (0.001 SOL, inside the fee ceiling) to the real-shape AGTR buy and refuses at prepare with `UNCLASSIFIED_INSTRUCTION`; the mirrored pool→wallet sell transfer is also refused (it surfaces as `INTENT_SIGNERS_MISMATCH` — a pool-sourced transfer makes the pool a required signer — either way the build is refused at prepare, before any signing).

### P1-1 — settlement payouts: a separate, visible, bounded effect — FIXED (fixtures 33, 34, 35; builds on 23)

**The design, in plain terms.** A bundled distributor settle pays out fee entitlements that the distributor program computes **on-chain** (balance × index − settled, under the S1 debt-accounting program); the gateway's build declares only that settles are bundled — it states no payout figure. An exact prepare-time payout therefore **cannot be derived without re-implementing the distributor's entitlement math against state the build doesn't carry**, so the model is **bounded, with both numbers on the card and bound into the approval token**:

- **Maximum** = the distributor vault's token balance read at prepare time. The vault is authenticated before use: the canonical token record must name the distributor state and vault (a settle-bundling trade on a token whose record doesn't → `SETTLEMENT_UNMODELED`), the settle instructions must name exactly that state and vault, and the vault account read from chain must be a token account of the card mint owned by that state (`SETTLEMENT_VAULT_UNREADABLE` otherwise).
- **Minimum** = 0. The build states no payout minimum and a settle may legitimately pay 0 (the live AGTR build's settles pay 0 today — no accrued entitlements); inventing a positive minimum would refuse legitimate trades.

**On the card**, as its own block (never netted silently into the swap delta):

> settlement: this trade bundles 2 distributor settle(s). Payouts are computed on-chain from holder entitlements and cannot be known exactly in advance; the approved bound is the distributor vault's balance at prepare time: vault `6LeucG…` — total payouts to the 2 settled holder account(s) (one of them yours) must be ≤ 934 raw units, paid only to the settle-named accounts, and the swap legs are checked NET of any payout to you. The bound is part of the approval token.

The full model (vault, state, min, max, the settled holder accounts, the settle count) is inside the **policy fingerprint (policy v2)**, and send re-derives the settle set from the fresh build and refuses on any disagreement (`SETTLEMENT_MODEL_MISMATCH`).

**Recipients are pinned to the production forms** at classification. The distributor program itself enforces on-chain that a settle's recipient is a token account *of the named holder* for the card mint, so a payout can only ever reach the holder's own accounts; the runtime pin keeps every payout inside the modeled accounts:

- the **wallet's own** settle must pay the wallet's derived token account for the mint — no exceptions, because the send-time decomposition reads that account (this also closes a round-3 gap: the recipient account was not previously pinned at all);
- a **counterparty** settle (the crank the gateway bundles for the swap's other side) may pay that holder's derived account, a canonical pool token vault, or the account the transaction's own validated venue swap names as its token vault — the curve pool's base vault is a venue-program account the published record does not name, and the swap trading against it is validated independently. (Observed follow-up, outside this PR: the gateway's launch records carry `baseVault`/`quoteVault`/`poolAuthority`; the published canonical record does not. Publishing them would let the runtime pin this form from the record alone.)

**At send**, against the simulation of the exact signed bytes: the vault may only lose tokens, and its total outflow must be ≤ the approved maximum (`SETTLEMENT_PAYOUT_EXCEEDS_MAX`); payouts to accounts whose only role is receiving one are each bounded to [min, max]; **sells** decompose exactly — (token delta of the wallet's account + approved amountIn) must itself lie in [min, max], and the swap leg is then checked exactly as before; **buys** check the swap leg **net** — (token delta − total payouts) ≥ approved minimum — so a payout can never mask a short swap. Documented conservative edge: on a buy, a very large counterparty payout combined with a near-minimum swap output can refuse a trade that would have been acceptable gross; this fails closed by design rather than netting silently.
